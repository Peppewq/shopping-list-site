Shopping List

Shopping lists grouped by shop, for Android

Shopping List is an Android app for keeping shopping lists grouped by shop: the supermarket, the greengrocer, the Asian shop. Checked items move to a shared "Checked" list, ready to be added back next time.

Features

  • Groups for each shop, with items you can check, move and reorder
  • Notes on items, highlighted so you don't miss them
  • Items linked across shops: buy it in one, and it disappears from the others
  • Suggestions from previously checked items
  • Undo and redo
  • Interface in English and Italian, light and dark theme, adjustable text size
  • Optional backup to the user's own Google Drive

Your data

Shopping List has no server and no account of its own. The list stays on the phone. The only data that leaves the phone is the optional backup, which is written to the user's own Google Drive. Read the privacy policy for details.
