Privacy policy

Shopping List · Last updated: October 26, 2026

Shopping List ("the app") is an Android app for keeping shopping lists grouped by shop. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

Data stored on your phone

The app stores the groups and items you enter (names, notes, which shops an item belongs to, and whether it was checked), and your preferences for language, theme and text size. This data is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. The app has no server and does not send this data anywhere, except for the optional Google Drive backup described below.

Android may include the app's data in your phone's own Google backup, as it does for other apps, depending on your phone's backup settings.

Google Drive backup

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app asks Google for permission to use the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. With this permission the app can only create and update files that it created itself; it cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

The app uses this permission only to keep one file in your own Google Drive up to date: shopping-list-backup.json, a copy of your lists, in a folder named "Shopping List". When you choose "Restore from Drive", the app reads this file back.

To show which account is connected, the app reads your name and email address from Google Drive. They are stored only on your phone and are removed when you sign out.

The backup file is stored in your Google account and is subject to Google's own terms and privacy policy. You can delete it from Google Drive at any time, and you can remove the app's access from your Google account settings at any time.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect analytics, usage statistics or crash reports.
  • It does not show ads and contains no advertising or tracking libraries.
  • It does not read your contacts, email or any Google data other than its own file and your account name and email address.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.

Google API services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored on the phone. The backup file in Google Drive stays there until you delete it yourself. Signing out in the app's Settings also removes the app's access to your Google Drive.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
